Changes: independently parsed selected timestamp and ECG/BVP columns;
retained exact parsed source values and source-relative timestamps in
contiguous excerpts of at most 60 seconds. No filtering, interpolation,
normalization or NASA processing scripts. Display-only min/max decimation
retains extrema; packaged excerpts retain all selected samples.

Each excerpt records its full source object key, full source SHA-256,
original sample count and half-open original sample offset. ECG marks
retain original workbook provenance and percentages mapped against the
ORIGINAL number of samples, not elapsed time or excerpt length. Display
shades the strict interior of floored bounds. Endpoint-contact benchmark
windows were omitted. Marks describe author heartbeat-detector exclusions,
not diseases or complete artifact truth. Unmarked is not certified clean.
BVP has no supervised quality annotations or classifier.

Transformations: independent detector and fixed features from the first
600 seconds of the selected ECG lead; annotated beat counts provide the
engineering error target. Person-separated training, calibration and test;
correlated noise variants reserved for stress reporting. Data-only scaler
and logistic coefficients carry frozen-plan provenance. No raw MIT-BIH
recordings or development-time WFDB/numerical libraries are bundled.
The model concerns detector count error, not disease, astronaut health,
clinical confidence or mission clearance. NASA-domain acceptance is withheld.
The model's own deployment field and experiment report govern its limited
benchmark status. No PhysioNet, MIT, hospital or NASA endorsement is implied.
