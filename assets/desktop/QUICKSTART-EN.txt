BioTwin Sync - Astronaut Health Monitoring
Portable Windows hackathon prototype

START
Extract the whole ZIP and run BioTwinSync.exe. Keep _internal beside the EXE.
No Python installation, login, API key or internet is needed for bundled use.
A four-step guide stays readable until you continue. Back, Next, Finish and
Close guide are available. Reopen it from Help > Quick guide. It does not
change your crew, measurements or session. Help also offers Reduce motion.

THREE AREAS
Open Crew 001 or Crew 002 from Crew overview.
1. Your condition: read the graphical status and next action first.
   View measurements opens Head / cognition, Heart / signals and supplied
   measurements. Back to condition restores the simple view. Chart help
   explains sources, axes, shading and unavailable values.
2. Next step: open the onboard assistant or Review received steps. The full
   checklist opens with scrolling and fixed completion, repeat and follow-up
   controls. Guided examples cannot record actions. Old notes are cleared
   when crew, session or evidence changes.
3. Earth medical link: compare Crew now and Earth last received, observation
   times and evidence age. Earth review reveals the
   delivered reading and Messages / Review / References. These group replies,
   Flight Surgeon authoring and historical NASA cases. Only delivered evidence
   reaches the Earth view. Hide Earth review returns to the simpler display.

Use Cognition, NASA recordings or Measurements beside the crew figure,
or select its matching target. Alt+H opens cognition; Alt+C opens recordings.
The figure is navigation, not an organ-health map. Session > Onboard assistant
remains available when a received checklist is the main action.
Subtle blue light supports the crew figure; repeated gradient rows are removed.
Sunset orange emphasizes actions; light-blue traces show observations.
Green means no detected change in the comparable recorded reaction check;
other vitals have no change assessment here. It never means healthy or cleared.
Amber asks for review, red marks a reported urgent concern, and
neutral means more evidence is needed. Words and symbols accompany colour.
Motion marks actual interface changes and simulated delivery. Source replay
starts only when you press Play; Reduce motion stops automatic transitions.

GUIDED DEMO
Open Demo controls, then 09:00 Sent, 09:20 Earth, 09:30 Change, 09:40 Guide.
At 09:30 the crew reading changes but Earth still sees the older reading.
At 09:40 the received conditional checklist selects Plan B.
Automatic playback stops and holds at 09:40. Next extended event explicitly
explores the later example completions, repeat and follow-up. One timeline
contains playback, speed and the slider.
Moving packets show simulated delivery, not a real network.

Why this result? and Inspect received snapshot open readable evidence first:
source, observed/received times, quality and the actual descriptive reference.
Technical JSON is a separate disclosure. The NASA trace banner identifies
reference recordings as separate from this crew's observations.

LOCAL INPUT
Choose Session > Local measurements, then Start empty local session.
This replaces the working session; save first if needed.
Measure records supplied vitals or a sleep diary. Review Source, time &
context before recording. Quality defaults to review; usable is your own
attestation. Timed reaction check uses Space; cancelled/focus-lost or
unfinished checks are not saved. It is not WinSCAT or a readiness test.
Signals imports ECG/BVP JSON and offers marks/repeat requests. Open separate
model demo keeps NASA example inference outside your personal history.
History includes Export import examples. Imports validate before mutation.

ONBOARD ASSISTANT
From Next action, Open onboard assistant. Choose a concern and answer Yes,
No or Unsure in Questions, then review Guidance and Record assessment.
Add a note is optional; text is retained verbatim, not medically interpreted.
Record actions only after performing them. Reassess when evidence changes.
Completed actions do not mean symptoms resolved; prior urgent reports remain.
More opens sources, answers/reasons, crew history, Earth received handoff
and link simulation. Send consultation explicitly queues the handoff.
Get urgent help and reported urgent warnings work before saving or sending.
The program does not contact help or perform real medical communication.

SESSIONS AND REVIEW
Session contains Save/Open/Export, New operator session and Reset guided
scenario. Ctrl+S saves; Ctrl+O opens. Choose the matching crew before import.
Save/export before replacing a workspace. Existing saved files stay intact.
To author a conditional checklist: send consultation, advance delivery,
open Earth review > Review > Author Plan A / B, then deliver the reply. Complete
the appropriate steps and Send follow-up. No step is automatically completed
by choosing its branch. Session files have a 2 MiB limit and are not encrypted.

READ THE DATA CORRECTLY
The source label distinguishes synthetic mission data, local input and NASA
recordings. Graph bands describe earlier-history spread, not healthy ranges.
NASA SOTERIA traces are recorded pilot-simulator signals, not live astronaut
sensors; channel clocks are independent. Amplitude units remain unverified.
Missing values stay unavailable. Sleep diaries are not sleep stages.
Two small NASA-trained forests are experimental signal-review aids:
injected-corruption balanced accuracy 98.63%; natural author-exclusion
balanced accuracy 44.16% (failed). Real recordings require manual review.
The assistant uses source-linked rules, not a trained diagnosis model.
Clinical review and flight approval are pending. It does not diagnose,
prescribe medication or clear tasks. NHS/FDA sources are terrestrial
background; the overdue fatigue-source review date is disclosed in Details.

References > Fetch NASA optionally downloads 26 known public WinSCAT files
(28,204 CSV bytes), verifies them and caches them. Internet is needed only
for this action; no health data is uploaded, and no login/key/payment is
required. Historical reference cases are not matched patients. Raw NLSP is
not included in this ZIP. Help > About / data sources contains attribution.

Offline automatic check (choose a new report path):
BioTwinSync.exe --smoke-test --report "C:\writable folder\smoke.json"
Exit 0 and status=passed indicate software checks, not clinical or visual
acceptance or testing on another computer. Read ATTRIBUTION.txt and
RUNTIME-NOTICES.txt. Keep normal Windows protection policies enabled.
